Explore the challenge ↓

THE IDEA

A common starting point.
What will you do with it?

Improve one component or rethink the whole system. The shared setup gives teams a way to compare approaches and uncover the roadblocks to reliable underwater manipulation.

The environment

A small-scale underwater vehicle-manipulator system, built on TU Hamburg’s ROS 2/Gazebo teaching framework.

Two ways to begin

Teleoperated and autonomous baselines to build on, from perception and planning to control and integration.

A shared measure

One manipulation task and common success metrics. The exact task, scoring and submission rules are still being defined.
